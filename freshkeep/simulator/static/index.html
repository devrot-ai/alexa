Privacy & safety

Data is stored only after explicit consent, on the server you run (JSON file or DynamoDB). Deletion is two-step (“delete my data” → explicit confirmation). Utterance text is never written to the audit log. AI-generated text is marked as estimate/advice, never as verified fact.

Before FreshKeep remembers anything

FreshKeep keeps a memory of your kitchen so Alexa+ can help you waste less food. To do that it would store:

  • what you bought, quantities, and expiry dates (estimated if you don't say one),
  • your shopping list and waste/rescue history.

Nothing is stored until you consent. You can withdraw consent and delete everything at any time (“delete my data”). This demo instance uses synthetic sample data.

Add groceries from a receipt

Paste the item lines (or the whole receipt — noise like totals and tax is ignored). With FRESHKEEP_TEXTRACT=1 you can also upload a photo (Amazon Textract). Nothing is stored until you confirm the parsed items.
